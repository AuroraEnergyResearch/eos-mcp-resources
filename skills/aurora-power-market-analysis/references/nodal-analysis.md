# Nodal / locational analysis

Some markets model locational (node-level) prices. The structure below is consistent across the markets that have it (verified on ERCOT, CAISO, MISO, NYISO, PJM; ISO-NE and Chile also carry it) but the specifics vary by market - confirm them from the options rather than hardcoding. ERCOT is the worked example throughout.

- **Detect nodal via `scenario_explorer_list_scenarios`, not the regions list.** A region's `products` array in `scenario_explorer_list_regions_and_currencies` does NOT reliably show the nodal product - several markets that have nodal don't list it there. Confirm by listing scenarios for the region and looking for `modelOutputType: nodal_output` (product `*_nodal`, e.g. `erc_nodal`, `cas_nodal`, `pjm_nodal`).
- **Nodal and market/PRMF are separate scenario records.** Resolve "latest Central" *per output type* - the newest Central overall may be a `market_output` scenario that carries no nodal files, while the latest `nodal_output` is a separate (sometimes slightly older) record. Prices come from the nodal scenario; commodities and capacity are NOT in any nodal scenario - pull them from the vintage-matched market/PRMF scenario.
- **File layout.** Per-node series use the `{currency}-{nodename}-nodal-1h.csv` template (pass `node_name`); the combined `{currency}-nodal-1m.csv` / `-nodal-1y.csv` carry ALL nodes with a `Node` column (filter to your node - these are large, all-node files). Find the node's exact ID and its `Region`/zone by filtering the combined annual file.
- **Columns.** Nodal ATC price = `Baseload price`; a `Baseload price basis` column sits alongside it. Nodal basis = nodal ATC - zonal ATC; the portable way to get zonal is `zonal = Baseload price - Baseload price basis`, which works in every nodal market. A `nodalsystem` system/zonal-reference file exists in some markets (ERCOT, CAISO) but not others (MISO, NYISO, PJM), so don't depend on it - the file's own basis is the reliable reference. Cross-check the reconstructed zonal against the market's hub baseload when you can.
- **What varies by market:** storage-spread durations (e.g. CAISO 4h/6h/8h vs MISO/NYISO/PJM 2h/4h/8h), sub-hourly real-time granularities (CAISO 5M+15M, ERCOT 15M, some markets none), forecast start year and default currency.
- **Attribution limits.** Commodities (gas/coal/carbon, from the market scenario) move the price LEVEL - both nodal and zonal together - but do NOT explain the basis. The basis is local congestion, and node/zone-local capacity entries/exits are NOT published in the nodal outputs of any market checked, so basis movements can be characterised qualitatively but not decomposed against local build/retirement from this data. Say so rather than implying a capacity driver you can't evidence.
